Privacy

Short version: no accounts, no cookies, no ad trackers. Last updated 31 October 2026.

What stays on your device

The site keeps a few things in your browser, and nowhere else:

  • Your saved tools, so the Saved page can list them (localStorage).
  • Reviews you wrote, so you can see them while they wait for a person to read them (localStorage).
  • The row order you chose on the compare page (sessionStorage, gone when you close the tab).

None of this is sent to us. Clearing your browser data deletes it.

What you send us

Only what you type into a form and choose to submit: the newsletter, Submit a tool, Write a review and Contact forms. That is the fields on the form, such as your email and your message. We use them for the purpose of that form, for example to reply to you or to review a submitted tool. Reviews and tool submissions that we publish may show what you wrote, but never your email.

Forms are delivered through a third-party form service, which processes the submission on our behalf. We don't sell or share your details for advertising.

Third parties that load with the page

  • Google Fonts. The page loads its typefaces from Google, which means your browser contacts Google's servers and your IP address is visible to them.
  • Vendor sites. "Visit website" leaves AutomationFindr. Once you're there, the vendor's own privacy policy applies.

Analytics

There are no analytics scripts on the site today. If we add privacy-friendly analytics later, this page will say so before it ships.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete something you sent us, write through the contact form and tell us which submission it was. To remove what is stored on your device, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes with it, and the changelog mentions it.
