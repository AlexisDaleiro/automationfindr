Real data and actions

  • All tools now load from data/tools.json, each with its official site and a pricing record: plans, the date we checked, and the page we used.
  • "Visit website" goes to the vendor's real site.
  • Saved tools, with Compare from the Saved page.
  • Newsletter, Submit a tool and Write a review forms. Reviews wait for a person to read them before they appear.
  • Removed numbers on the home page that we couldn't back up.
