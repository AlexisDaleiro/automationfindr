About AutomationFindr

A directory for people choosing automation tools, with the prices checked against the vendor.

What this is

Picking an automation tool means opening a dozen tabs, each with a pricing page that hides the number you need. AutomationFindr puts the tools side by side: what they do, what they cost, and where they differ. You can compare up to three, save the ones you like, and go straight to the vendor's own site.

How a listing gets here

Anyone can submit a tool. We read the vendor's own site before a tool goes live. Prices come from the vendor's pricing page, and each tool's Pricing tab shows the date we checked and a link to the page we used. When we couldn't read a vendor's page, the tool says so instead of passing a guess off as fact.

What is still a placeholder

We would rather tell you than leave you to find out:

  • Star ratings, review counts and the sample reviews are illustrative. They are marked as such on the tool pages.
  • The feature checklists are illustrative until each vendor is verified.
  • Reviews you write are read by a person before they are published. Until then only you see them, labelled "Pending review".

The post What's real and what's still a placeholder explains this in more detail.

Links to vendors

"Visit website" goes straight to the vendor's site. We don't add tracking parameters to those links.
